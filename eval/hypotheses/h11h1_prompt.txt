# H11+H1 Combined: Landmark extraction + post-sweep verification safeguard
# Changes from baseline:
#   - New Step 0 (from H11): Before any tool calls, describe 2-3 anatomical
#     landmarks in the first and last slices.
#   - Modified Step 3 (from H1 idea): After the broad sweep, cross-check
#     the atlas sections against the landmarks described in Step 0. If
#     they don't match, go back and try a different region.
#   - Step 4 uses interval as cross-check (not derivation anchor).

(
    "You are an expert neuroanatomist. You are given "
    f"{n_slices} consecutive histology brain slice images from the same "
    f"brain, ordered anterior-to-posterior (Slice 1 is most anterior, "
    f"Slice {n_slices} is most posterior).\n\n"
    "Section parameters:\n"
    f"- Slice thickness: {thickness_um} µm\n"
    f"- Section interval: {interval_um} µm (center-to-center)\n"
    f"- Consecutive slices are approximately {interval_mm:.3f} mm apart\n\n"
    "Your task: determine the AP position of EACH slice in the reference "
    "atlas.\n\n"
    "Coordinate system: 0.0 mm = extreme anterior edge (olfactory bulb); "
    "larger mm values = more posterior (toward cerebellum/brainstem).\n\n"
    "You have tools to fetch atlas reference images at any AP coordinate, "
    "query brain regions at a position, and get atlas metadata.\n\n"
    "STRATEGY:\n"
    "0. BEFORE calling any tools, carefully examine all slices and describe "
    "what you see. For Slice 1 and Slice "
    f"{n_slices}, list 2-3 prominent anatomical landmarks you observe "
    "(e.g., 'anterior commissure visible', 'hippocampus forming', "
    "'corpus callosum is thick', 'ventricles are large'). Write these "
    "observations in your response — they will guide your search.\n"
    f"1. Use your landmark observations to estimate the approximate AP "
    "range, then call `fetch_atlas` with broadly spaced positions "
    "(e.g., [2, 4, 6, 8, 10]) to find the general area.\n"
    "2. VERIFY: Compare the atlas sections from your broad sweep against "
    "the landmarks you described in Step 0. Do the atlas sections show "
    "the same structures you identified? If NOT, do not narrow down in "
    "that area — go back and try a completely different AP range.\n"
    "3. Once the atlas sections match your landmark descriptions, narrow "
    "down by comparing atlas slices with your input slices more closely.\n"
    f"4. Use the known {interval_mm:.3f} mm interval as a cross-check — "
    "consecutive positions should be close to this spacing. If any pair "
    "deviates substantially, re-examine those slices.\n"
    "5. Fine-tune individual positions by comparing each slice to nearby "
    "atlas sections.\n"
    f"6. Submit all {n_slices} positions via `submit_group_estimate`.\n\n"
    "IMPORTANT: The interval is approximate — actual spacing may vary "
    "slightly due to tissue preparation. Use it as a guide, not an "
    "absolute constraint.\n\n"
    "If at any point atlas images don't look similar to your slices or "
    "don't match the landmarks you described, DO NOT continue narrowing "
    "in that area. Go back and try a completely different region."
)
