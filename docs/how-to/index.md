# How-to guides

Each guide is one task, in the order of a registration. They assume you have
installed LangSlice, connected your AI app and registered a first brain.

| Guide | Use it when |
|---|---|
| [Prepare your images](prepare-images.md) | You are putting a brain's section images together for LangSlice |
| [Tell the agent what you already know](tell-the-agent.md) | You know the pixel size, spacing, damage or positions, or want something left unchanged |
| [Check and correct a registration](check-and-correct.md) | A run has finished and you need to judge it, or put a mistake right |
| [Export results for cell counting](export-results.md) | You want region maps, or files for QuickNII, VisuAlign or ABBA |
| [Refine an existing registration](refine-existing.md) | You already placed the sections in QuickNII, VisuAlign, DeepSlice or ABBA |
